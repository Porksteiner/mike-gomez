The problem

Questions

Before you ask.

Does anything go to the internet?

Your prompts and documents do not. They are processed on the machine in your office. I use a separate secure connection to maintain the system, and you can switch that connection off whenever you like.

How does it compare to ChatGPT?

For drafting, summarizing, and working with your own files, it is very useful. On the most difficult reasoning tasks, the largest cloud models are still stronger. The trade is capability at the top end for full control of your data.

What do we need on our side?

A power outlet, a network port, and a place to put a computer about the size of a desktop tower. No server room and no IT department.

What if we stop the care plan?

The machine is yours and keeps working. You stop receiving updates, new models, and support.

Do you work with firms outside Alabama?

Yes. The system ships to you and setup is finished remotely, followed by a video training session.
